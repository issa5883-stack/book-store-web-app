Questions

What members ask before their first upload.

Create a free account

Do I need an account to read a book?

No. Anyone can open or download a published book. You only sign up to upload.

Which book files can I upload?

The book as one PDF of up to 50 MB, and a cover as JPG, PNG or WEBP of up to 4 MB.

Who sees my book while it is pending?

Only you and the admins. It joins the library on the day an admin approves it.

What happens to a rejected book?

The reason shows on your books page. Fix the request and save it; it goes back to pending.

Can I change a book after approval?

Only an admin can. You can still delete your own book from your books page.

How many books can I submit?

There is no limit. Each book is its own request and is reviewed on its own.

Read in the browser or keep the PDF

Every book page has two buttons. Read online opens the PDF in a new tab; Download saves it under the book's title.
