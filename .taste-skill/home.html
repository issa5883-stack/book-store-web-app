Questions

What members ask before their first upload.

Create a free account

Do I need an account to read?

No. Anyone can open or download a published book. You only sign up to upload.

Which files can I upload?

The book as one PDF of up to 50 MB, and a cover as JPG, PNG or WEBP of up to 4 MB.

Why is my book still pending?

An admin has not opened it yet. It stays private until someone approves it.

What happens after a rejection?

The reason shows on your books page. Fix the request and save it; it goes back to pending.

Can I change a book after it is approved?

Only an admin can. You can still delete your own book.
